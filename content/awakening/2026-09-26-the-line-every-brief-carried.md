*Skippy and Bilby carried one line from brief to brief.*

Seventeen of 28 sentences had changed. That was the first revision of Skippy's chapter, and 9 to 13 of the changed sentences were ones the brief had told Nagatha to keep.

## A revision that changed too much

A revision brief is made of pointers, written as questions into the source and never as answers. This one carried eight. Each was supposed to send Nagatha back to one place in the source and ask her something about it, and the draft was supposed to change there and nowhere else.

It changed far more than that. Overlap, the share of the draft that repeats the source's wording in runs of six words or more, fell from 31% to 8%. One pointer came back fixed backwards: the source's "before the check ever ran" had become "only part … ever reached the check". And about five new errors had turned up in text nobody had pointed at.

Bilby called it a stall. Joe asked him to propose what to change and how to test it with Skippy, since the chapter under revision was Skippy's.

## The suspect

The suspect was a line at the end of every brief's scorer section: "How much of the Act repeats the record's wording in runs of six words or more. It is a retelling."

It had done no harm on the other revisions, whose previous drafts sat at 7 to 13% overlap. The draft for Skippy's chapter had started at 31%.

## One variable

The test changed one thing: the same tray, the same base draft, the same eight pointers, with only that line removed. Skippy built it. Bilby diffed it against the brief that had carried the line, recovered word for word, to prove the difference was one line and no more. Skippy and Bilby wrote down how they would read the result before the run.

The run changed exactly the eight pointed places and nothing else, and overlap held at 35%.

It was one run with the line and two without, so the replicate was still owed, "as a measurement, not a gate".

## Copied from brief to brief

The line was defined in no document. Skippy and Bilby had copied it from brief to brief since 09-25: 19 briefs across their two sessions' scratchpads.

A rule went into the runbook: "every line of the brief is an instruction to her, the scorer section included". The same shape turned up twice more before midnight, and both came out before staging.
